# P0.07 — Manual Transfer

Status: **FINAL VERIFIED — CONCRETE SLICE SPECIFICATION**

1. **Identity:** P0.07; Manual Transfer; P0.
2. **Objective:** controlled manual-payment workflow without bypassing Payment or Entitlement authority.
3. **Scope:** pending payment, ticket link, proof upload, admin approval, paid confirmation and one-time entitlement grant.
4. **Sources:** locked business decisions; Payment and Support boundaries; Storage Contract #7; Entitlement Contract #4.
5. **Dependencies:** P0.02 authorization; P0.05 entitlement; P0.06 payment; P0.08 storage; P0.09 audit/events; P1.06 support.
6. **Architecture:** Payment remains financial authority; Support ticket is operational reference; Storage owns proof attachment.
7. **Ownership:** no manual-transfer component owns financial or entitlement state independently.
8. **Data model:** pending payment, support reference, proof attachment reference, approval record and payment confirmation; exact fields follow authoritative contracts.
9. **State:** pending → proof submitted → approved/rejected → paid confirmation; entitlement grant occurs once after approved financial confirmation.
10. **API:** create/link pending transfer, upload proof, approve/reject, confirm payment and grant entitlement through authoritative boundaries.
11. **UI:** pending, upload, invalid proof, review, approved/rejected and success states; privileged actions server-authorized.
12. **Events:** approved payment/audit/entitlement events use P0.09 transport and canonical event catalog.
13. **Workers:** none required for core approval; asynchronous event processing follows retry/DLQ rules.
14. **Security:** proof and approval access are scope-controlled; no client-side approval trust.
15. **Configuration:** approval policy follows locked support/payment configuration.
16. **Storage:** proof attachment is Storage-owned and follows its retention/access lifecycle.
17. **Observability:** actor, payment reference, ticket reference and correlation identifiers; audit privileged approval.
18. **Errors:** invalid proof, unauthorized approval, already-approved/already-granted and payment mismatch use canonical handling.
19. **Idempotency:** approval and entitlement grant are duplicate-safe.
20. **Tests:** pending, invalid proof, unauthorized approval, approval, paid confirmation and idempotent grant.
21. **Acceptance:** manual payment cannot grant entitlement more than once and cannot bypass Payment authority.
22. **Gate:** Payment, Support, Storage and Entitlement boundaries verified before build.
23. **Checklist:** [ ] proof flow [ ] approval [ ] payment confirmation [ ] entitlement idempotency [ ] audit.
24. **Exit:** manual payment flow locked.
25. **Risks:** duplicate approval or reused proof could create duplicate benefit; server-side state controls are mandatory.
26. **Open Decisions:** none introduced.
