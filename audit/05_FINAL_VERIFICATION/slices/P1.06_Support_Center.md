# P1.06 — Support Center

Status: **FINAL VERIFIED — CONCRETE SLICE SPECIFICATION**

1. Identity: P1.06; Support Center.
2. Objective: support ticket/service workflow with explicit ownership and auditability.
3. Scope: ticket lifecycle, references, SLA/status and authorized operational actions.
4. Sources: Support addendum; locked support policy; Payment/Storage boundaries.
5. Dependencies: P0.02 authorization; P0.08 Storage; P0.09 audit/events; P0.06 Payment.
6. Architecture: Support owns ticket/service workflow; Payment owns financial state.
7. Ownership: ticket, status, service references and support actions; attachments remain Storage-owned.
8. Data model: ticket, status, references, SLA metadata, attachment references and audit actions.
9. State: ticket lifecycle and status transitions follow locked support policy.
10. API: create/update/read ticket, attach references, authorized operational actions.
11. UI: ticket list/detail, status, SLA, attachment and error/access states.
12. Events: ticket/status/audit events through P0.09.
13. Workers: SLA/notification work may be asynchronous and retry-safe.
14. Security: ticket visibility/actions are role/scope controlled server-side.
15. Configuration: approved SLA/support configuration only.
16. Storage: attachments use Storage lifecycle; Support stores references.
17. Observability: ticket/action/actor/correlation identifiers.
18. Errors: unauthorized access, invalid transition, missing reference and attachment failure.
19. Idempotency: operational actions and notifications must tolerate retry.
20. Tests: access scope, attachment lifecycle, status transitions and auditability.
21. Acceptance: Support cannot directly alter Payment or Entitlement financial state.
22. Gate: support/payment/storage boundaries verified.
23. Checklist: [ ] ticket lifecycle [ ] scope [ ] references [ ] attachments [ ] audit [ ] tests.
24. Exit: support center boundary ready.
25. Risks: privileged support action could bypass financial ownership.
26. Open Decisions: none introduced.
