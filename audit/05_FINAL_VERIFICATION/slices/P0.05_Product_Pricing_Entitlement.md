# P0.05 — Product / Pricing / Entitlement

Status: **FINAL VERIFIED — CONCRETE SLICE SPECIFICATION**

1. **Identity:** P0.05; Product/Pricing/Entitlement; P0.
2. **Objective:** authoritative sellable-product, price and granted-right boundary.
3. **Scope:** product definition/version/market availability; pricing; entitlement grant, reservation, commit, release, reversal, lock/unlock and adjustment. Out: authorization semantics.
4. **Sources:** Core Contract #4; locked business decisions; Phase 4 SoT; Phase 5 corrections.
5. **Dependencies:** P0.00, P0.02, P0.04, P0.06, P0.09.
6. **Architecture:** Product/Price defines sellable commercial state; Entitlement owns granted/consumed right.
7. **Ownership:** Product/Price owns sellable definition; Entitlement owns usable benefit and consumption state.
8. **Data model:** Product, ProductVersion, Price, market availability, Entitlement, reservation/commit/release/reversal records; exact fields remain Contract #4 authority.
9. **State:** product/version availability; entitlement grant → reserved → committed/released/reversed/locked/unlocked as defined by lifecycle authority.
10. **API:** product/pricing lookup and entitlement grant/consume/lock/unlock/reservation operations; authorization precedes mutation.
11. **UI:** product/price visibility and entitlement state; loading, unavailable and error states; no UI-only access control.
12. **Events:** approved product and entitlement lifecycle events; P0.09 owns transport/delivery.
13. **Workers:** asynchronous entitlement/event processing follows idempotent retry/DLQ rules.
14. **Security:** authorization and entitlement are separate; feature access requires applicable entitlement plus authorization.
15. **Configuration:** approved market/pricing configuration only; cannot override contract or authorization.
16. **Storage:** persist versioned product/price and entitlement state with historical traceability.
17. **Observability:** actor/correlation, product version and entitlement operation identifiers; no sensitive data.
18. **Errors:** unavailable product, invalid price/currency, authorization failure and invalid entitlement transition use canonical errors.
19. **Idempotency:** grant/reservation/commit/release/reversal must prevent duplicate benefit consumption.
20. **Tests:** IDR/USD pricing, versioning, market availability, grant/consume, lock/unlock, reservation/reversal, historical traceability and duplicate protection.
21. **Acceptance:** commercial definition and entitlement lifecycle remain separated, traceable and idempotent.
22. **Gate:** all transitions trace to Contract #4/lifecycle authority before build.
23. **Checklist:** [ ] schema [ ] lifecycle [ ] API [ ] authorization [ ] events [ ] persistence [ ] tests.
24. **Exit:** commercial entitlement boundary reusable by downstream slices.
25. **Risks:** duplicate grant or stale version can create incorrect commercial benefit.
26. **Open Decisions:** none introduced; locked business decisions remain authoritative.
