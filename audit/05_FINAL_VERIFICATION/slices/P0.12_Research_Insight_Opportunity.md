# P0.12 — Research Insight / Opportunity

Status: **FINAL VERIFIED — CONCRETE SLICE SPECIFICATION**

1. Identity: P0.12; Research Insight/Opportunity.
2. Objective: transform canonical research evidence into traceable insight/opportunity outputs.
3. Scope: evidence-backed insight, opportunity, confidence, freshness, score, recommendation and approved Planner/Analyzer handoff.
4. Sources: Research authority; Core Contract #10; Phase 4 SoT.
5. Dependencies: P0.11 Research foundation; P0.13 Planner; P0.14 Analyzer.
6. Architecture: derived interpretation references Research evidence without taking source ownership.
7. Ownership: insight/opportunity output and provenance linkage; Research remains source authority.
8. Data model: insight, opportunity, evidence references, confidence, freshness, score and recommendation.
9. State: generated → reviewed/saved → approved downstream handoff as defined by source authority.
10. API: generate/read/save/handoff operations; no unsupported metric creation.
11. UI: insight/opportunity evidence, confidence/freshness and recommendation states.
12. Events: approved insight lifecycle events only.
13. Workers: analysis generation may be asynchronous and retry-safe.
14. Security: workspace/tenant authorization applies to evidence and derived output.
15. Configuration: scoring/freshness settings only where approved.
16. Storage: persist derived insight with references to canonical evidence.
17. Observability: run, source, evidence and correlation identifiers.
18. Errors: insufficient evidence, stale data, failed generation and invalid handoff are explicit.
19. Idempotency: repeated generation/save must not duplicate the same logical output.
20. Tests: evidence, confidence/freshness, score, save and downstream handoff.
21. Acceptance: every insight is evidence-backed, traceable and contains confidence/freshness context.
22. Gate: no invented metrics or unsupported recommendation semantics.
23. Checklist: [ ] evidence [ ] provenance [ ] output state [ ] handoff [ ] tests.
24. Exit: insight boundary locked.
25. Risks: derived output without provenance can mislead downstream production.
26. Open Decisions: none introduced.
